# User Testing Log – ShortlistAI

> ✏️ **Marc: fill this in after testing the LIVE app with 2–3 real people** (HR staff, recruiters, or anyone who has hired). Each session takes about 10 minutes. Use incognito on their device.

## Test script
1. Open the live URL. Click **Load sample data**, then **Screen 4 candidates**.
2. Ask: "Is the #1 candidate who you would pick? Why or why not?"
3. Ask them to open a candidate card and check 2–3 evidence quotes against the resume. "Are they real?"
4. Have them paste one job description of their own and 2 resumes (they can remove names first).
5. Ask: "What was confusing? What's missing? Would you use this for your next role?"
