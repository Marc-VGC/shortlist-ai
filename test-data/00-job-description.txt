JOB TITLE: Recruitment Specialist (Makati City, Hybrid)

About us
We are a 150-employee IT and customer-experience outsourcing company in Makati. We plan to hire 120 people next year across customer support, technical support and admin roles, and we need a Recruitment Specialist to own the hiring pipeline end to end.

What you will do
- Manage end-to-end recruitment for 10-15 open roles at a time: sourcing, screening, interviews, job offers and onboarding hand-off
- Source candidates on LinkedIn, Jobstreet, Indeed, Kalibrr and Facebook job groups
- Run initial phone screens and coordinate interviews with hiring managers
- Keep our applicant tracking system (ATS) up to date
- Prepare weekly recruitment reports (time-to-fill, source of hire, pipeline status) in Excel or Google Sheets
- Organize job fairs and campus recruitment events

Requirements
- At least 2 years of end-to-end recruitment experience
- Experience with high-volume hiring (at least 15 hires per month)
- Hands-on experience with an applicant tracking system such as Manatal, Workable, Zoho Recruit or Greenhouse
- Strong sourcing skills on LinkedIn and Jobstreet
- Proficient in Excel or Google Sheets for recruitment reporting
- Working knowledge of Philippine labor law basics (DOLE regulations, pre-employment requirements)
- Excellent written and spoken English

Preferred
- Experience hiring for BPO or IT roles is a plus
- HR certification (e.g. CHRP, SHRM-CP) or a degree in Psychology or HR is an advantage
- Experience running job fairs is a plus

Work setup: Hybrid (3 days onsite in Makati), day shift.
