Classic Chicken Adobo (serves 4)

Ingredients:
- 1 kg chicken thighs and drumsticks
- 1/2 cup soy sauce
- 1/3 cup cane vinegar
- 1 head garlic, crushed
- 3 bay leaves
- 1 tsp whole black peppercorns
- 1 cup water

Steps:
1. Marinate the chicken in soy sauce and garlic for 30 minutes.
2. Brown the chicken in a pan, then add the marinade, water, bay leaves and peppercorns.
3. Simmer for 30 minutes. Add the vinegar and do not stir for 5 minutes.
4. Simmer until the sauce thickens. Serve with steamed rice.
